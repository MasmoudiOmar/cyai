# Ethics policy

cyai helps people **prepare** for AI-run interviews. It is not a tool for cheating.

**We will not build, and will not merge:**

- anything that captures, transcribes or listens to a live interview or meeting;
- overlays, hidden windows or "copilots" that suggest answers during a real interview;
- features designed to evade an employer's integrity or proctoring checks.

**We will build:** realistic simulations, honest feedback, and transparency about how AI interviewers tend to work. Interviewer profiles are based on public descriptions of interview formats. They are not affiliated with any vendor and do not claim to reproduce any vendor's proprietary scoring.
